# 🎨 Phase 03C: Art Direction & Visual Concept System

## 1. Visual Storytelling & Conceptual Metaphors

The Creative Director reasons like a Senior Art Director, translating abstract marketing problems into physical scenes:

* **Problem Scene (*Leads Boncos*):**
  - **Focal Subject:** Sales manager reviewing unread leads in modern marketing gallery.
  - **Environment:** Maket arsitektur properti di latar belakang.
  - **Lighting:** Directional twilight with subtle warm glow.
  - **Color Mood:** Obsidian navy with rose-red alert accents.
* **Infrastructure Insight (*Akses Tol & Capital Gain*):**
  - **Focal Subject:** Modern elevated toll flyover connecting planned residential township.
  - **Lighting:** Golden hour sunset with warm reflections on asphalt and glass.
  - **Color Mood:** Slate navy, indigo, and warm sunset amber.
* **Listicle (*Kesalahan Follow-Up*):**
  - **Focal Subject:** Modern geometric multi-story facade with rhythmic louvers.
  - **Lighting:** Diffused overcast evening light.
  - **Color Mood:** Midnight navy with warm champagne gold accents.

## 2. Multi-Variant Art Direction (1–3 Variants)

1. **Variant A: Cinematic Hero Editorial:**
   - Full photographic immersion with dark twilight mood and high-contrast typography.
2. **Variant B: Minimalist Authority Editorial:**
   - Deep obsidian monochrome aesthetic (`DEEP_OBSIDIAN`), enhanced contrast, cooler color bias.
3. **Variant C: Layered Editorial Composite:**
   - Warm golden sunset atmosphere (`PREMIUM_GOLD`) with structured metric overlays.
